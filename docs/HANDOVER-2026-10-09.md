## What DevPulse is

DevPulse checks every pull request on GitHub and gives it a risk score (Low, Medium, High, Critical), so the team knows which changes need the most careful review.

## How to invite a teammate today

1. Add them as a collaborator on **devpulse-test-repo** on GitHub.
2. In DevPulse, open **Members** → **Invite people** → enter their email.
3. Click **Copy link** and send it to them (WhatsApp, Slack, email).
4. They open the link and click **Continue with GitHub**.

The link works **once** and expires after **24 hours**.
